## Progressive stages

1. History and presenting concern.
2. Symptoms, vitals, and focused assessment.
3. Relevant labs and diagnostics.
4. Orders/treatments and response.
5. Change in condition and reevaluation.

Reveal only the next stage when appropriate. Ask: What matters? What is dangerous? What do you assess or do next? Why? How will you evaluate the result?
